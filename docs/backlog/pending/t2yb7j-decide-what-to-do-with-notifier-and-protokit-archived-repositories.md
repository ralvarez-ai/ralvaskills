Both repositories are archived (read-only), so their `go.ralva.dev` commit (`notifier` v0.2.0, `protokit` v0.3.0 were prepared) exists only locally and was not tagged. Either unarchive, push and tag them, or retire the modules and remove them from `MODULES` in `go-vanity`. `protokit` was absorbed by `contracts`; `notifier` is listed in the vanity Worker today.

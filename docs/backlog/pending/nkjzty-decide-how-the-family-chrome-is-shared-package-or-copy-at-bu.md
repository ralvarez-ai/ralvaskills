`FamilyMark`, the credit footer, the section rail and the provenance `<details>` are copied in six sites (ralvafi, ralvagym, ralvapass, ralvasend, ralvadocket, rsk). Land the credit in every site first, then choose a shared package or a `sync-shared.mjs`-style copy (ralvafi has one). A package couples six release cycles; copy-at-build does not.

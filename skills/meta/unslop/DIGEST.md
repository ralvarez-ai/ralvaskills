# Unslop (digest)

Numbered rules that strip AI patterns from prose. Rule ids are stable so other skills can cite them.

## Always-on rules
- Cut filler and chatbot phrases: no "-ing" tails that add no fact, no praise openers, no generic conclusions, no "I hope this helps".
- Plain words: "use", not "leverage"; "is" or "has", not "serves as"; no AI vocabulary (delve, pivotal, showcase) and no abstract metaphor nouns (surface, harness, primitive).
- Name sources, mechanisms and numbers; delete vague attributions and sentences that fit any project.
- One idea per sentence, active voice, whole sentences, one hedge at most.
- No em or en dashes: use a period or comma, or a spaced hyphen (` - `); ranges use `2-4`.
- Sentence case headings, straight quotes, no decorative emojis, no bold-label echoes.

## Hard constraints
- Preserve meaning and tone; edit, do not rewrite content.
- Do not apply to code, quoted output or text another tool parses.

Invoke the full skill when: editing or reviewing prose, docs, commit messages or reports, or when another skill says to write "through unslop".
